# Cirqen manual: department in-charges (NIC)

You see your own department's equipment and work orders only.

## 1. First sign-in

Sign in with the one-time password from your HOD, choose your own password,
and draw your signature (a finger works on a phone). You approve work with
this signature.

## 2. Report a faulty machine

Report faults to your maintenance workshop the way your hospital normally
does (phone, fault book, or in person). The technologist who attends opens
the work order in Cirqen for that machine. Never give patient names or
details in a fault report.

To check a machine's status, next PPM and calibration, scan the QR label on
it with a phone and sign in.

## 3. Approve completed work

When the workshop finishes a repair or service, it waits for you.

- On a phone: menu, **Work Orders, Approve (phone view)**. Each waiting work
  order shows the machine, the fault and what was done. Check the machine is
  working, sign with your finger, and tap **Approve**; or **Decline** and say
  why.
- On a computer: **Work Orders, Create Work Order** lists the work orders
  waiting for your approval, with the same Approve and Decline buttons.

Approve only after you have checked the machine is back in working order.
Your approval and signature go on the work order and in the audit trail.

## 4. Your dashboard

Your department's equipment and its status, open work orders, and when each
machine's next PPM and calibration are due.

## 5. Certificates

**Calibration, Certificates** lists the calibration certificates for your
department's equipment. Scan the QR code on a printed certificate to check it
is genuine.
