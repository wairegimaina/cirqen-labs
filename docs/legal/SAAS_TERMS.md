# Cirqen service terms (draft)

> **Draft.** Not legal advice. For review by a Kenyan advocate. These sit
> alongside the EULA (software) and the DPA (personal data), and cover the
> HQ service and support that Cirqen Labs runs.

1. **Service.** Cirqen Labs provides: HQ (sync between the Customer's sites,
   certificate numbering, public certificate verification, software
   updates), and support as in the Support Schedule below.
2. **Fees.** [Per site per year / per equipment item], invoiced [annually in
   advance]; payable within 30 days. For county governments, payment terms
   follow the procurement contract. Late fees: [ ].
3. **Availability.** HQ target: 99.5% per calendar month, excluding planned
   maintenance announced 48 hours ahead (normally outside 07:00–18:00 EAT).
   Sites keep working when HQ or the internet is down; changes sync later.
   If a month falls below target, the Customer gets a service credit of
   [5%] of that month's fee per full 0.5% below, up to [25%].
4. **Support.** See Support Schedule (`SUPPORT.md`): hours, severity levels
   and response times.
5. **Customer duties.** Keep sign-in details safe; enter no patient data;
   keep the site server or PCs, backups' second copy and network in working
   order; install updates.
6. **Security and data.** As in the DPA. Cirqen Labs backs up HQ daily and
   tests restores quarterly.
7. **Suspension.** Only for non-payment after 30 days' written notice, or
   to stop a security threat (with notice as soon as possible). Suspension
   of HQ does not stop the sites working locally.
8. **Term and exit.** [12] months, renewing unless either side gives [60]
   days' notice. On exit, the data export and deletion in the DPA apply;
   the Customer may keep using its local copy read-only to consult records.
9. **Changes.** Cirqen Labs may improve the service; it will not materially
   reduce it during a paid term. Changes to these terms take effect at
   renewal with 60 days' notice.
10. **Liability, law, disputes.** As in the EULA.
