# Data processing agreement (template)

> **Draft** for review by a Kenyan advocate before use.

Between **[Hospital]** ("Controller") and **Cirqen Labs [legal name,
registration number, address]** ("Processor").

1. **Subject.** The Processor hosts and supports the Cirqen equipment
   management system for the Controller. Annex 1 describes the data.
2. **Instructions.** The Processor processes personal data only on the
   Controller's documented instructions, which are this agreement and the
   Controller's use of the software, unless Kenyan law requires otherwise
   (in which case it tells the Controller first, unless the law forbids it).
3. **Confidentiality.** Everyone at the Processor with access is bound by
   confidentiality.
4. **Security.** The Processor keeps the measures in Annex 3 and does not
   reduce them without notice.
5. **Sub-processors.** The Controller authorises those in Annex 2. The
   Processor gives 30 days' notice of any change; the Controller may object
   on reasonable grounds. The Processor imposes the same obligations on them
   and remains responsible for them.
6. **Transfers outside Kenya.** The Controller consents to the transfers in
   Annex 2 under the safeguards stated there, in line with sections 48–50 of
   the Data Protection Act, 2019.
7. **No patient data.** The Controller will not enter patient information.
   If any is found, the Processor tells the Controller and it is removed.
8. **Data subject requests.** The Processor passes any request it receives
   to the Controller within 5 working days, and helps the Controller answer.
9. **Breaches.** The Processor tells the Controller without undue delay, and
   in any case within 24 hours of becoming aware, with the information the
   Controller needs to notify the ODPC within 72 hours.
10. **Assistance.** The Processor helps with impact assessments and ODPC
    consultations related to the service.
11. **Audit.** The Processor provides the information needed to show
    compliance, and allows one audit a year on 30 days' notice.
12. **End of service.** Within 30 days the Processor gives the Controller a
    full export (database dump and issued certificate files), then deletes
    the Controller's data from HQ and backups within 90 days, and confirms in
    writing. The site's own server or PCs remain the Controller's.
13. **Liability** is as in the main service agreement.
14. **Law.** Laws of Kenya; courts of Kenya.

**Annex 1: data.** Staff users (name, username, email, phone, role,
workshop/department, signature image, sign-in history with IP address);
supplier contacts (name, phone, email); records of work done. Purpose:
equipment maintenance, calibration and quality records. Duration: the term
of the service agreement.

**Annex 2: sub-processors.** [Application hosting provider, region];
[managed PostgreSQL provider, region]; [error monitoring provider, region,
only if enabled]; [email provider]; [SMS provider, e.g. Africa's Talking,
Kenya]. Safeguards: provider data processing terms; encryption in transit
and at rest; access restricted to named Cirqen Labs staff.

**Annex 3: security measures.** See section 6 of `DATA_PROTECTION.md`.
