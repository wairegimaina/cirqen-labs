# Cirqen end-user licence agreement (draft)

> **Draft.** Not legal advice. A Kenyan advocate must review this before it
> is used. Bracketed items are to be completed.

This agreement is between **Cirqen Labs [legal name, registration number,
address]** ("Cirqen Labs") and the organisation installing Cirqen (the
"Customer"). Installing or using Cirqen means the Customer accepts it.

1. **Licence.** Cirqen Labs grants the Customer a non-exclusive,
   non-transferable licence to install and use Cirqen at the sites and for
   the number of users in its order, for the Customer's own equipment
   management, for the term of its subscription.
2. **Restrictions.** The Customer will not sell, sublicense or rent Cirqen;
   remove notices; or, except as the law allows or clause 3 permits,
   decompile or modify it.
3. **Open-source components.** Cirqen includes components under their own
   licences, listed in "Third-party licences" (in the app and installer).
   Those licences govern those components. In particular, nothing in this
   agreement restricts the Customer from modifying the LGPL-licensed
   libraries included (such as Qt/PySide6) for its own use, or from reverse
   engineering to debug such modifications.
4. **Customer data.** The Customer's data stays the Customer's. Where
   Cirqen Labs processes it (HQ sync, support), the data processing
   agreement applies.
5. **Updates.** Cirqen installs updates signed by Cirqen Labs. The Customer
   will not block security updates for more than 30 days.
6. **Calibration results.** Cirqen computes results from the procedures,
   tolerances, standards and readings the Customer enters. The Customer is
   responsible for those inputs, for validating the software for its use
   (Cirqen Labs provides a validation pack), and for its certificates.
7. **Warranty.** For 90 days from first delivery, Cirqen will perform
   substantially as described in its documentation; Cirqen Labs will fix or
   replace it, or refund the fees for the affected period. Otherwise, to the
   extent the law permits, Cirqen is provided "as is".
8. **Liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential
   loss. Cirqen Labs' total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12
   months before the claim. Nothing limits liability for death or personal
   injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
9. **Termination.** Either party may end this agreement if the other
   materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 30 days of
   notice. On termination the Customer stops using Cirqen; Cirqen Labs gives
   an export of the Customer's data as the DPA says.
10. **Law.** Laws of Kenya. Disputes go first to good-faith negotiation for
    30 days, then to [mediation / arbitration under the Nairobi Centre for
    International Arbitration rules / the courts of Kenya].
