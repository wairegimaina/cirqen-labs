# Privacy notice for Cirqen users

> **Draft** for review by a Kenyan advocate. Replace the bracketed items.

**Who we are.** Cirqen is the equipment management system used by
[hospital name] (the "hospital"). The hospital decides how your information
is used (it is the data controller). Cirqen Labs [registered address]
provides and supports the software and stores a copy of the records on its
servers on the hospital's behalf (it is a data processor).

**What we hold about you.** Your name, username, work email and phone
number, role and workshop or department; your signature image if you sign
work orders or certificates; your sign-in history (time, IP address,
browser); and the work you record (work orders, maintenance, calibrations).
We do not hold patient information, and you must not enter any.

**Why.** To let you sign in, to assign and approve work, to send you
reminders, to keep the quality records the hospital must keep (including
for ISO/IEC 17025), and to keep the system secure. The legal basis is the
hospital's legal obligations and its legitimate interest in managing its
equipment safely (section 30 of the Data Protection Act, 2019).

**Who sees it.** People at the hospital, according to their role; Cirqen
Labs support staff when needed to help you; and the hosting providers
Cirqen Labs uses, some of which store data outside Kenya under safeguards
set out in the hospital's agreement with Cirqen Labs.

**How long.** Your account stays while you work at the hospital. Your name
remains on quality records you signed for as long as those records must be
kept, usually at least five years. Sign-in history is kept for 12 months.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see the information held about you, to
correct it, to object to its use, or to have it deleted where the law
allows (signed quality records must be kept). Ask the hospital's data
protection officer [contact], or Cirqen Labs at [email]. You may also
complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).
