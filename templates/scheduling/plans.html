How a plan schedules

Each group gets the months it is worked in. Each description gets how often it is due.

A device on a 6-month interval in a February/August group is due every February and August. On 12 months it is due in one of the two, spread evenly across devices. A device whose interval can't fit its group's months is listed with the reason rather than guessed.

Completed schedules never move. When a new version is activated, open schedules that no longer fit are moved, and work already under way is listed for review.
