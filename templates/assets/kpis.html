How these are counted
  • Failure: a Repair work order that was not declined.
  • MTBF: machine-days in the period divided by failures.
  • MTTR: average time between a repair's recorded start and finish.
  • Uptime: 100% minus recorded repair hours as a share of machine hours. Waiting for a repair is not recorded, so this is an upper bound.
  • PPM done: completed PPM schedules out of those due in the period up to this month.
