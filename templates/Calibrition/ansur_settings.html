{# ── In Ansur, once ──────────────────────────────────────── #}

In Ansur, once

  • Add a device (DUT) field named Cirqen Job: Tools > Options > Ansur Preferences > DUT, double-click the last field. Cirqen fills it in and uses it to match a record to its job.
  • Turn Restrict Access off (Tools > Options > General), or Cirqen cannot read the records.
  • Sign in to Ansur with Disable Electronic Signature ticked until a signed record has been tried here.
  • Keep device serial numbers the same in Ansur and Cirqen.
